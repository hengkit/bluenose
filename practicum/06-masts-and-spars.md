## 1. Shaping and Tapering Masts and Spars

For each spar:

1. Identify it on Sheet 4.
2. Record the plan length/profile.
3. Mark taper stations.
4. Remove material gradually.
5. Rotate the spar frequently.
6. Check straightness by rolling it on a flat surface.
7. Check diameter/profile with calipers.
8. Sand to final finish.

## 2. Building & Installing the Masts

1. Finish mast fittings before installation where access will later be difficult.
2. Dry-fit foremast.
3. Check rake and centerline.
4. Dry-fit mainmast.
5. Compare both masts from bow/stern.
6. Install only after alignment is correct.
7. Recheck after cure.

## 3. Bowsprit & Jibboom

1. Shape bowsprit.
2. Prepare heel/fittings.
3. Dry-fit.
4. Check centerline.
5. Install.
6. Shape/install jibboom.
7. Check alignment and future rigging clearance.
