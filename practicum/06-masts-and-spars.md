## Bench standard

**Identify → measure from plan → dry-fit → inspect → glue → cure → inspect again.**

If a measurement or fitting location in this practicum differs from the plans, **the plans win**.

## 1. Shaping and Tapering Masts and Spars

1. Identify each spar on Sheet 4.
2. Record its plan length and diameter/profile.
3. Mark taper stations.
4. Remove material gradually.
5. Rotate the spar frequently.
6. Check straightness by rolling it on a flat surface.
7. Use calipers where appropriate.
8. Finish to the plan profile.

## 2. Building & Installing the Masts

1. Finish each mast and its required fittings.
2. Dry-fit the foremast.
3. Check centerline and rake.
4. Dry-fit the mainmast.
5. Compare both masts from bow and stern.
6. Install only after alignment is correct.
7. Recheck after cure.
