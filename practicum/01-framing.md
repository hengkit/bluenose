## 1. Bending Wood

### Before you start

- [ ] Sort the wood by thickness.
- [ ] Identify pieces that require bending.
- [ ] Test the bending method on scrap.
- [ ] Protect the work surface.

### Procedure

1. Wet/steam or otherwise soften the stock using the method recommended by the manual.
2. Bend gradually around a former or against the hull shape.
3. Let the wood dry and stabilize before installation.
4. Compare the curve with the plan.
5. Reject pieces with crushed or split grain.

### QC

- [ ] Correct curve.
- [ ] No split grain.
- [ ] Part sits naturally without springing away.

## 2. Center Keel Assembly

### Procedure

1. Lay the keel pieces on a flat building board protected with wax paper or plastic.
2. Mark the **center/reference line and bulkhead stations on both sides** of the center keel.
3. Transfer dimensions with a tick strip or dividers rather than relying on a single measurement.
4. Dry-fit the keel pieces.
5. Apply wood glue.
6. Align the reference line with a straightedge.
7. Hold the assembly flat while the glue cures.
8. Allow the joint to cure fully before loading the keel.

### QC

- [ ] Reference line continuous.
- [ ] Bulkhead stations correct.
- [ ] Keel straight.
- [ ] No twist.
- [ ] Joint fully cured.

## 3. Installing the Sternpost

1. Dry-fit the sternpost to the keel.
2. Compare its profile with the plan.
3. Check centerline alignment.
4. Glue.
5. Recheck after cure.

## 4. Cutting the Rabbet

1. Transfer the rabbet from the hull plan.
2. Start with shallow cuts.
3. Remove material gradually.
4. Work from the bearding line toward the keel edge.
5. Test the rabbet with scrap plank material.
6. Check the angle at several stations.
7. Do not deepen the rabbet to rescue a poorly fitted plank.

### QC

- [ ] Rabbet follows the plan.
- [ ] No overcutting.
- [ ] Planking has a clean landing.

## 5. Tapering the Stem

1. Transfer the stem taper from the plans.
2. Remove material gradually and symmetrically.
3. Preserve the rabbet.
4. Check the stem against the plan profile.

### Bulkhead bevel preparation — do this BEFORE installing bulkheads

The manual has you transfer the bevels from the plans with a tick strip and mark them on the bulkheads. Cut the major bevels with a #11 blade. Leave the small amidships deck/side bevels for final sanding after installation.

- [ ] Bulkheads A–O identified.
- [ ] Bevels transferred.
- [ ] Major bevels cut.
- [ ] Subtle amidships bevels left for later sanding.
- [ ] No over-cutting.

## 6. Installing the Bulkheads

### 6A. Cut the bulkhead timberhead/stanchion extensions first

**This must happen before the bulkheads are installed.**

The bulkheads are 3/16" thick; the timberhead/bulwark-stanchion extensions are reduced to **1/8"**.

1. Lay out bulkheads A–O.
2. Confirm each identity against Sheet 1.
3. Mark the cut line.
4. Cut/sand the extension to the specified thickness.
5. Keep the cut square.
6. Do not remove material from the main bulkhead body.

> Do not confuse this with the later **Remaining Bulwark Stanchions** operation. Those additional stanchions between bulkheads are installed later in Stage 1.

### 6B. Dry-fit every bulkhead

1. Test-fit each bulkhead in its keel slot.
2. If a slot is tight, lightly sand the slot.
3. Do not force the bulkhead.
4. Check the bulkhead reference line against the keel reference line.

### 6C. Install and align

1. Glue the bulkheads in the manual sequence.
2. Match reference lines.
3. Use a machinist square to check perpendicularity.
4. Tack temporary battens just below the deck.
5. Measure port-to-starboard spacing.
6. Sight along the battens from bow and stern.
7. Adjust while glue is workable.
8. Let cure.

### 6D. Bearding line and fairness

The bottom of each bulkhead must feather onto the bearding line. The top of each bulkhead at centerline should be flush with the center keel.

After installation:

1. Check the bearding line.
2. Check centerline height.
3. Use the recommended 1/8" square basswood batten to check fairness.
4. Sand bumps.
5. Shim dips with appropriate wood rather than creating hollows.

### Critical QC gate

- [ ] All bulkheads correct.
- [ ] Stanchion extensions reduced to 1/8".
- [ ] Bevels prepared.
- [ ] Reference lines aligned.
- [ ] Bulkheads square.
- [ ] Bearding lines correct.
- [ ] Centerlines flush.
- [ ] Temporary battens show a fair hull.
- [ ] No twist or banana shape.

**Do not continue until this gate passes.**

## 7. Stern Blocks & Transom Framing

1. Dry-fit stern blocks.
2. Check centerline.
3. Check stern profile.
4. Install transom framing.
5. Allow to cure.
6. Recheck the transition into the bulkheads.

## 8. Horn Timbers

1. Identify the horn timbers.
2. Dry-fit.
3. Check port/starboard symmetry.
4. Glue.
5. Recheck stern geometry.

## 9. Covering the Mast Slots

1. Install the specified mast-slot covering pieces.
2. Keep the mast openings at their plan dimensions.
3. Test-fit the mast openings after cure.

## 10. Great Beam & Deck Beam

1. Identify the Great Beam and deck beam.
2. Dry-fit.
3. Check deck geometry.
4. Glue.
5. Recheck after cure.

## 11. Waterway

1. Dry-fit the waterway around the deck edge.
2. Follow the deck sheer.
3. Work in manageable sections.
4. Check the waterway against the bulwark stanchions.
5. Clean squeeze-out.
6. Preserve the slight bevel required on the innermost plank.

## 12. Knightheads & Hawse Timbers

1. Locate both assemblies on the plan.
2. Dry-fit as a complete bow group.
3. Check centerline and symmetry.
4. Preserve the hawse openings.
5. Install.

## 13. Main Rail

1. Pre-bend the rail stock.
2. Dry-fit the complete run.
3. Check sheer.
4. Check its relationship to the stanchions.
5. Glue progressively.
6. Recheck the curve while the adhesive is workable.

## 14. Buffalo Rail, Monkey Board & Monkey Rail

1. Dry-fit the stern components.
2. Check symmetry.
3. Install in manual order.
4. Compare the completed stern against the plan.

## 15. Remaining Bulwark Stanchions

These are the **additional stanchions between bulkheads**, not the bulkhead extensions prepared in Step 6.

1. Locate positions on the plan.
2. Prepare stock to the specified size.
3. Mark positions.
4. Dry-fit.
5. Install vertically.
6. Check spacing.
7. Recheck rail alignment.
