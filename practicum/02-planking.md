## Bench standard

**Identify → measure from plan → dry-fit → inspect → glue → cure → inspect again.**

If a measurement or fitting location in this practicum differs from the plans, **the plans win**.

## 1. Getting Started

Before the first plank:

- [ ] Stage 1 quality gate passed.
- [ ] Bulkheads are fair.
- [ ] Rabbet is ready.
- [ ] Plank stock sorted.
- [ ] Bending method tested.

### Fairing

1. Use a long sanding block.
2. Remove only high spots.
3. Test a thin strip against several bulkheads.
4. Work until the strip follows a continuous curve.
5. Check bow, midships, and stern separately.

## 2. Planking Battens and Belts

1. Mark the plank layout from the manual/plan.
2. Establish temporary belts/battens.
3. Check how many planks arrive at the bow and stern.
4. Spread tapering across the run.
5. Correct the layout before cutting permanent planks.

## 3. Planking Butts

1. Study the manual's butt-layout example.
2. Mark intended butt locations.
3. Stagger adjacent joints.
4. Avoid accidental ladder joints.
5. Keep the pattern consistent with the plans.

## 4. Spiling

1. Make a paper template.
2. Transfer the hull curve.
3. Transfer the template to plank stock.
4. Cut slightly oversize.
5. Sand to fit.
6. Dry-fit the complete plank.
7. Check the neighboring plank transition.

## 5. Fastening the Planks

Practice first:

- [ ] Adhesive control.
- [ ] Temporary pin/clamp placement.
- [ ] Butt-joint alignment.
- [ ] Squeeze-out cleanup.

## 6. Planking the Outer Hull

For each plank:

1. Identify its belt and run.
2. Determine taper from the plan.
3. Bend/shape the plank.
4. Dry-fit the full length.
5. Check every bulkhead contact.
6. Check bow and stern landings.
7. Check butt position.
8. Apply adhesive.
9. Install without forcing.
10. Clean squeeze-out.
11. Let cure.
12. Inspect the next run.

### Bow checkpoint

A plank that buckles or twists at the bow needs correction before gluing. Fix the taper/bend rather than forcing it.

### Stern checkpoint

Maintain a controlled run into the stern. Do not create unnecessary wedges or scraps.

## 7. Planking Inboard — Ceiling Planks

- [ ] Identify ceiling-plank runs.
- [ ] Dry-fit around structural members.
- [ ] Preserve openings.
- [ ] Install.
- [ ] Clean adhesive.

## 8. Planking the Decks

**Deck planking belongs to Stage 2.**

1. Establish the deck centerline.
2. Study the deck-plank/butt layout.
3. Mark openings.
4. Dry-fit a short section.
5. Install the reference plank.
6. Work outward while maintaining width.
7. Keep the butt pattern consistent.
8. Fit carefully around hatches/openings.
9. Finish the perimeter/margin treatment shown on the plans.
10. Allow the deck to cure before final sanding.
