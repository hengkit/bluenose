## 4. Treating the Lines

1. Test the manual's treatment on scrap.
2. Treat standing line consistently.
3. Treat running line consistently.
4. Avoid over-stiffening.
5. Keep line texture appropriate to scale.

## 5. Belaying Lines

Sheet 6 is the primary belaying reference.

For each running line, record:

- source;
- blocks;
- fairleads;
- final belaying point.

Do this before installing the line.

## 6. Rigging Tools

Prepare:

- fine scissors;
- tweezers;
- fine pliers;
- needle/rigging tools;
- magnification;
- beeswax/line treatment;
- fine cutting tools.

## 7. Blocks & Deadeyes

1. Clean holes.
2. Verify size.
3. Prepare stropping.
4. Match deadeye pairs.
5. Prepare lanyards.
6. Orient blocks/deadeyes according to the plan.

## 8. Sailmaking

1. Identify every sail.
2. Transfer outlines from the plans.
3. Cut accurately.
4. Add hems/seams/details called for.
5. Mark attachment points.
6. Test-fit.
7. Store finished sails safely until needed.

## 9. Rigging Without Sails

If you choose a no-sail model, follow the manual's no-sail guidance. Do not simply delete structural rigging.
